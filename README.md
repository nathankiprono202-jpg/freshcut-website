# FreshCut Barbershop

## Welcome to FreshCut

FreshCut is a modern website concept for a neighborhood barbershop. It uses a clean black-and-gold design to create a premium look while making important information easy to find.

Whether you're exploring the website as a visitor or considering a similar website for your own business, this project demonstrates how a simple online presence can help a local business showcase its services.

**Please note:** FreshCut is a demonstration project, not an actual barbershop. The listed prices, location, opening hours, and WhatsApp contact are examples and should not be treated as verified business information.

## What Can You Do on the Website?

### Explore the homepage

Get a first impression of the FreshCut brand, its style, and its services.

### View the services

Explore three example services:

- Regular Haircut — KSh 300
- Skin Fade — KSh 500
- Haircut & Beard — KSh 700

These prices are illustrative and may not reflect the prices of any real barbershop.

### Learn about FreshCut

Discover the values behind the concept, including precision, personal style, and customer comfort.

### Find contact information

View the example location and opening hours, and explore the WhatsApp contact links.

The WhatsApp links are part of the demonstration and may use a placeholder number. Please do not rely on them to contact a real business.

## How to Use the Website

You don't need to install anything or create an account.

1. Open the website using the link below.
2. Use the navigation menu to visit Home, Services, About, or Contact.
3. Scroll through the page to explore the different sections.
4. Open the website on a phone, tablet, or computer to see how the layout adapts to different screens.

**[Open FreshCut Website](https://nathankiprono202-jpg.github.io/freshcut-website/)**

## Designed for Different Devices

FreshCut is designed to work across:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Desktop and laptop computers

The layout adjusts to different screen sizes to make the website easier to browse.

## Interested in a Website Like This?

FreshCut is also an example of how a local business can present itself online.

A customized website like this could help a real barbershop display its services, share its prices, provide directions and contact information, and make it easier for customers to get in touch.

The design and content can be adapted to suit a real business's branding and needs.
